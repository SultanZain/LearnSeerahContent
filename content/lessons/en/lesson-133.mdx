## A Remarkable and Culminating Year

Following the significant events of Tabuk examined in the previous lessons of this unit, and building upon the momentum of the conquest of Mecca and the victories at Hunayn examined throughout the previous units of this section, a remarkable phenomenon began to unfold across the entirety of the Arabian Peninsula — one that would come to be known as the Year of Delegations.

{/* page */}

## Tribes Arriving From Every Direction

Delegation after delegation arrived in Medina during this period, representing tribes from every corner of Arabia, each coming to declare their acceptance of Islam. This represented a fundamental and historically remarkable shift from the pattern this entire curriculum has traced since its earliest sections — decades of persecution in Mecca examined throughout Section 4, extended military conflict throughout the entire Medinan period this section has documented in such detail, tribe after tribe requiring either military engagement or careful diplomatic negotiation to address their relationship with the growing Muslim community.

Now, tribes arrived voluntarily, without the need for military campaign or extended negotiation, seeking to embrace the very message that had once required such extensive effort and sacrifice to establish and defend.

## The Quran's Own Description

This remarkable phenomenon receives direct and celebrated attention in the Quran itself, in a surah revealed specifically to address this culminating moment of the Prophet's ﷺ mission:

<Quran surah="110" ayah="1-3" />

*"When the victory of Allah has come and the conquest, and you see the people entering into the religion of Allah in multitudes, then exalt Him with praise of your Lord and ask forgiveness of Him. Indeed, He is ever Accepting of repentance."*

This passage connects the widespread and voluntary acceptance of Islam directly to the completion of the Prophet's ﷺ mission, and it instructs him, at this moment of unprecedented and historic triumph, not toward celebration of personal achievement, but toward glorification of Allah and continued seeking of forgiveness — echoing precisely the same posture of humility this curriculum examined in Unit 27 regarding his bowed head as he entered Mecca.

## A Reception of Warmth and Patient Teaching

The Prophet ﷺ received each of these arriving delegations with genuine warmth, generosity, and careful and patient instruction, taking personal time and attention to teach each group the fundamentals of the faith they had come to embrace, rather than treating their arrival merely as a matter of political consolidation or conquest to be administratively processed.

This reception reflects the consistent character this curriculum has traced throughout his entire life — from the gentleness within his own household examined in Unit 21, through the general amnesty extended to Mecca examined in Unit 27, to this culminating moment where the fruits of decades of patient struggle arrived, voluntarily and in overwhelming numbers, at his own door.

## Completing an Arc That Began in Section 1

Stepping back to consider this remarkable year within the fullest possible context this curriculum has established, the Year of Delegations represents the historically documented and visible completion of an arc that began with our very first lessons in Section 1 of this entire curriculum.

We examined there the ancient dua of Ibrahim <span className="font-ar">عليه السلام</span> and Ismail <span className="font-ar">عليه السلام</span>, asking Allah to raise from among their descendants a Prophet who would recite His verses and restore pure worship of the One God. We examined the finality and universal scope of Muhammad's ﷺ Prophethood, sealing the entire chain of Prophets we traced from Adam <span className="font-ar">عليه السلام</span> onward. We examined, in Unit 24 of this section, his direct and deliberate outreach to the great empires of the surrounding world, demonstrating that this universal understanding of his mission was acted upon during his own lifetime rather than merely claimed by later generations.

The Year of Delegations stands as the visible and historically documented fruit of this entire arc — the peninsula that had once required decades of persecution, exile, battle, and patient diplomacy to bring toward the truth now arriving voluntarily, tribe by tribe, to embrace the very message its own ancestors had once fought so fiercely to suppress.

## A Grief That Turned to Danger

In the aftermath of the Khaybar campaign examined throughout this unit, amid the genuine losses and displacement the fighting had caused among the people of Khaybar, a woman named Zaynab bint al-Harith prepared a meal for the Prophet ﷺ and presented it to him — a meal that contained poison.

{/* page */}

## Understanding the Motivation Honestly

Consistent with this curriculum's commitment to presenting difficult historical episodes with complete and honest context, it is worth understanding what motivated this action, according to Zaynab's own later account.

She had lost family members during the fighting at Khaybar — the same military engagements examined in detail throughout the previous lessons of this unit. Her grief and anger over these losses led her to this desperate and dangerous act, attempting to poison the very man she held responsible, in her own understanding at that time, for the destruction that had befallen her community and family.

This context does not excuse the action itself — a genuine attempt on human life, causing real harm as we will examine directly. But understanding the human grief and anger behind it, rather than presenting it as motiveless or inexplicable cruelty, reflects the same commitment to complete historical honesty this curriculum has applied consistently to every difficult topic examined throughout this section, including our earlier and equally honest treatment of Hind bint Utbah's actions at Uhud.

## Discovery and Genuine Consequence

The narrations describe the Prophet ﷺ receiving some form of indication regarding the meat's poisoned state — some accounts describe this as the meat itself somehow communicating a warning to him — prompting him to stop eating immediately and to warn the companions رضي الله عنهم who were sharing the meal with him.

This warning, however, came too late for everyone present. Bishr ibn al-Bara <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span>, who had already swallowed a portion of the poisoned meat before the warning was given, subsequently died as a direct result of its effects. This detail matters for understanding the genuine seriousness and real consequence of this incident — it was not a threat that passed without any actual harm, but a genuine act of attempted murder that resulted in the death of a sincere and innocent companion رضي الله عنه.

## A Measured Response to a Genuine Crime

When Zaynab bint al-Harith was brought before the Prophet ﷺ and questioned regarding her actions, she explained her motivation honestly, acknowledging what she had done and why, rather than attempting to conceal or deny her responsibility.

The historical accounts regarding the precise final resolution of her specific case show some variation among the different narrations preserved from this period. What remains consistent across these accounts, however, is the general character of the response: considered and measured rather than driven by immediate, uncontrolled anger, despite the genuine severity of what had occurred and the real loss of Bishr ibn al-Bara's <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> life as a direct consequence of her action.

## A Gathering Born of Necessity

In the hours immediately following the Prophet's ﷺ death, examined throughout this entire final unit, a gathering occurred at a location known as the Saqifah of Banu Sa'idah — a covered space belonging to this specific clan of the Ansar — where the urgent and genuinely necessary question of continued leadership for the Muslim community was addressed.

## Why This Question Could Not Wait

We have traced, across every unit of this entire section, the comprehensive scope of responsibility the Prophet ﷺ had carried simultaneously — political leadership, military command, judicial authority under the Constitution of Medina examined in Section 5, and religious guidance for a community that, following the Year of Delegations examined in Unit 29, now encompassed the entirety of Arabia.

This considerable scope of responsibility could not simply remain unaddressed, however genuine and profound the community's grief. Some form of continued leadership was a practical and urgent necessity for a community of this size and complexity, and it was this necessity that brought members of the Ansar, and subsequently the muhajirin, to gather at the Saqifah.

This proposal reflected a genuine and reasonable sense, given their extraordinary record of service, that their contribution merited serious consideration in determining how the community's leadership would continue.

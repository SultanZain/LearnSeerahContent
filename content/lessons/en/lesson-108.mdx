## A Response to Feared Tragedy

As we examined in the previous lesson, the delayed return of Uthman ibn Affan <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> from his mission into Mecca produced a rumor of genuine alarm within the Muslim camp at Hudaybiyyah: that he had been killed by Quraysh.

The Prophet ﷺ responded to this alarming possibility not with uncertainty or passive waiting, but with a decisive act that would become one of the most significant moments in the entire history of the early Muslim community.

{/* page */}

## Gathering Beneath the Tree

The Prophet ﷺ called his companions رضي الله عنهم to gather beneath a specific tree at the location of their encampment, and he called upon them to make a solemn pledge: that if the rumor of Uthman's <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> death proved accurate, and conflict with Quraysh consequently became unavoidable, they would fight to the death rather than abandon this cause or flee from what would follow.

This was not a pledge offered lightly or without genuine understanding of its implications. Every companion رضي الله عنه present understood precisely what they were committing to — a real possibility of death in the immediate confrontation this news, if true, would require.

Over a thousand companions رضي الله عنهم gave this pledge, one by one, without hesitation.

## Divine Commendation

The significance of this moment was affirmed at the highest possible level shortly afterward, through direct Quranic revelation:

<Quran surah="48" ayah="18" />

*Certainly was Allah pleased with the believers when they pledged allegiance to you, O Muhammad, under the tree, and He knew what was in their hearts, so He sent down tranquility upon them and rewarded them with an imminent conquest.*

This verse deserves careful attention for what it represents within the broader structure of the Quran. Direct, explicit, and collective divine commendation of a specific historical gathering of believers — named and honored by their action rather than addressed in general terms — occurs relatively rarely across the entire Quranic text. That this specific moment received such treatment reflects how profoundly significant Allah considered this particular demonstration of sincerity and commitment to be.

## Every Companion رضي الله عنه Present, Including Uthman رضي الله عنه

As we examined in detail in the previous lesson, the Prophet ﷺ ensured that Uthman <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span>, despite his physical absence during this pledge, was nonetheless included fully within it. He struck his own hand against the other, declaring this gesture to represent Uthman's <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> own pledge on his behalf.

This meant that when the Quran declares Allah's pleasure with those who pledged beneath the tree, this pleasure extends explicitly to Uthman <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> as well — a point of considerable significance given the previous lesson's direct refutation of any characterization suggesting he acted with anything other than complete loyalty and courage during this entire episode.

{/* page */}

## A False Rumor, A Genuine Pledge

Shortly after this remarkable pledge had been completed, Uthman <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> returned safely to the Muslim camp. The rumor of his death had proven to be inaccurate.

This resolution does not diminish the significance or sincerity of what had just occurred beneath that tree. Every companion رضي الله عنه who made that pledge did so believing, in that moment, that fulfilling it might genuinely require them to fight and die. Their commitment was real, tested against a genuine possibility of severe consequence, regardless of how the underlying circumstance was ultimately resolved. The sincerity of a pledge is measured by the conviction with which it is made, not retroactively discounted because the feared circumstance did not, in the end, materialize.

## A Standard for All Subsequent Generations

The Pledge of Ridwan has held a place of lasting and profound significance within Islamic memory and tradition, extending well beyond its immediate role in the specific crisis it addressed at Hudaybiyyah.

It stands as one of the clearest and most powerfully affirmed demonstrations of the collective sincerity, loyalty, and courage of the companions رضي الله عنهم — a generation whose character and devotion this curriculum has traced across every unit of this section, from the early acceptance of Islam under persecution examined in Section 4, through the battles and internal challenges examined throughout Section 6. This specific moment, elevated by direct Quranic commendation, provides a powerful and lasting reference point for understanding exactly what kind of commitment and character this remarkable generation of believers consistently demonstrated.

## What Remained to Be Settled

With this pledge made and Uthman's <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> safe return confirmed, the actual negotiations between the Muslim community and Quraysh's representatives continued. The atmosphere of the standoff had shifted meaningfully — Quraysh's own scouts and representatives, aware of the depth of commitment the pledge beneath the tree represented, understood with greater clarity than before that the Muslim community they faced was neither weak nor easily intimidated.

The specific terms that would ultimately be negotiated, terms that would appear, on their surface, to favor Quraysh considerably and would trouble many of the Prophet's ﷺ own companions رضي الله عنهم, form the subject of our next lesson — terms that the Quran itself would later describe, remarkably, as nothing less than a clear victory.

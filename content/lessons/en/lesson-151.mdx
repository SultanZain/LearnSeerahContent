## Naming an Origin Honestly

Classical Islamic historical scholarship identifies a figure named Abdullah ibn Saba as commonly associated with the earliest circulation of narratives specifically designed to incite hostility and suspicion toward prominent companions رضي الله عنهم, exploiting the genuine grief and political uncertainty following the events this final unit has examined.

Mainstream scholarship across different schools of Islamic thought has generally approached the more extreme narratives attributed to this figure and his associated movement with considerable and warranted skepticism — recognizing them as historically unreliable and, more importantly, fundamentally inconsistent with the extensive, documented, and evidence-based record of character and conduct this entire curriculum has traced across every section regarding the companions رضي الله عنهم in question.

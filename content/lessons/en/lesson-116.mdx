## A Promise Made the Night Before

As the Muslim forces engaged with the various fortified positions of Khaybar examined in our previous lesson, one particular fortress — known as Qamus — presented significant difficulty. Earlier attempts by other commanders had not achieved the breakthrough needed to bring this stronghold under Muslim control.

On the evening before what would become a decisive engagement, the Prophet ﷺ made a statement that generated considerable anticipation throughout the camp: *"Tomorrow I will give the banner to a man who loves Allah and His Messenger, and Allah and His Messenger love him. Allah will grant victory through him."*

This statement, promising both a specific outcome and a specific quality of the man who would achieve it, left many companions رضي الله عنهم genuinely eager to know who would receive this significant honor.

## An Extraordinary Feat at the Gate

Ali <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> proceeded toward the fortress with this commission, and the engagement that followed produced one of the most celebrated individual accounts of physical strength and courage preserved from the entire military history of the early Muslim community.

During the intense fighting at the fortress, Ali <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> lost or had his shield damaged, leaving him without this essential protection at a critical moment. In response, he is reported to have removed the fortress's own substantial gate — a heavy structure never designed to be lifted or carried by a single person — and used it as an improvised shield, protecting both himself and those fighting alongside him during the remainder of the engagement.

The scale of this feat is conveyed by the detail preserved in the narrations regarding what happened afterward: once the immediate need for this improvised shield had passed, the gate reportedly required the combined effort of several men to move it, a detail intended to convey just how extraordinary Ali's <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> individual strength and determination proved to be in that specific moment of need.

## Victory Fulfilled

Ali's <span className="font-ar">رضي الله عنه</span> conduct at this fortress achieved the decisive breakthrough that had eluded earlier attempts, fulfilling precisely the promise the Prophet ﷺ had made the evening before regarding a man beloved by Allah and His Messenger ﷺ through whom victory would be granted.

This success contributed significantly to the broader resolution of the entire Khaybar campaign, with the remaining fortified positions subsequently addressed and brought under Muslim control in the period that followed, bringing this extended and militarily complex campaign to its eventual and successful conclusion.
